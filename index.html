FAQ
Everything you need to know before you forge
Is $FORGE a real token?
+
Yes. $FORGE is a real ERC-20 token deployed on Base Network. Contract: 0x987D6851288e6F4798FDc7191b31b7b9513D361D. It is currently non-transferable until the ICO and DEX listing — all tokens earned now will be claimable at launch.
How do I claim 1,000 $FORGE free?
+
Open the mini app on Farcaster → go to Pool tab → click "Claim 1,000 $FORGE". A small gas fee (≈$0.05 in ETH on Base) is required to process the transaction onchain. First 5,000 users only — FCFS.
What is Base Network?
+
Base is a Layer 2 blockchain built by Coinbase on top of Ethereum. It offers near-instant transactions with gas fees as low as fractions of a cent. CastForge Arena is built on Base for speed, low cost, and reliability.
Do I need a wallet to play?
+
Farcaster's built-in wallet works automatically inside the mini app — no extra setup needed. You can also connect MetaMask or any Base-compatible wallet for transactions. Your Farcaster account is your identity.
What is the OG Mystery Box?
+
A limited collection of 666 boxes, each priced at $0.666 ETH. Open one to receive a random $FORGE reward — from 2,500 (Common) up to 35,000 (Mythic). At ICO price, a Mythic box is worth $38.50. Once 666 are gone, they're gone forever.
How does the KOL USDC program work?
+
Invite friends using your referral link. Hit milestones to earn real USDC — starting from 0.5 USDC at 10 referrals, up to 25 USDC at 1,500 referrals. Rewards are verified manually and sent directly to your wallet.
